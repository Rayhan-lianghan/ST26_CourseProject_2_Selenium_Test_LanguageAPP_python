About HSK / 什么是HSK

Chinese: 汉语水平考试（HSK）是一项 international 汉语能力标准化考试，旨在考查汉语非第一语言的考生在生活、学习和工作中使用汉语进行交际的能力。

English: The Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi (HSK) is an international standardized exam designed to evaluate the Chinese language proficiency of non-native speakers in daily, academic, and professional environments.
